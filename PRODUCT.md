# Mind Palace

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Private capture and retrieval of thoughts, reflections, memories, and book excerpts. Users write without maintaining a filing system, then search, browse emerging patterns, or ask questions grounded in selected thoughts.

## Users
Reflective knowledge workers, readers, writers, students, and founders. Source: ../PRD.md.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The signed-in homepage is a working app hub. Preserve capture, Ask, Recall, View thoughts, privacy controls, and sign out. Ask eligibility and per-thought AI consent remain product constraints. Cloud thought bodies are server-readable in the MVP; do not claim end-to-end encryption or operator blindness. Mobile-native and local-only storage remain deferred.

## Brand Commitments
The September 2026 homepage experiment uses a 3D brain as the central hub. On entry, the brain rises and action cards emerge from behind it. The user explicitly delegated the image style and requested a newly generated brain. The visual direction is exploratory, not an approved app-wide brand.

## Evidence on Hand
../PRD.md, current application behavior, and user-supplied brain and homepage references. The generated brain is decorative and contains no user content.

## Product Principles
- Capture with little friction.
- Retrieve with evidence from the user's own thoughts.
- Make AI consent and privacy controls understandable.
- Keep app controls usable on mobile and with reduced motion.
